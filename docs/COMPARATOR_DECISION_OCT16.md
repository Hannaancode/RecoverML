# Which RecoverML advantages are supported?

Updated 16 October 2026. This decision uses the full saved grids, including ties, slower cases and broader-loss controls.
It is an internal assessment, not external peer review or exhaustive novelty certification.

For the preservation use case, require complete exact recovery under the declared loss contract first,
then compare retained physical bytes among feasible stores, then compare recovery/planning cost.
This is an explicit use-case priority, not a fitted weighted score that makes every comparison a win.
A user who values partial coverage, ordinary pipeline iteration or fastest snapshot loading can prefer a different method.

The three defensible contribution categories remain: a precise conditional preservation contract,
an auditable exact retention/recovery implementation using established optimization methods,
and reproducible evidence showing its benefits and failure boundaries.
The file-export improvement is part of the systems implementation.
A new general optimization theory, universal dominance and independent human endorsement remain unestablished.

## Planner follow-up on 26 October

The [complete fixed follow-up](../results/research/planner_efficiency_oct26/RESULTS.md) preserves all 230 frozen optima and reports every slower case. Against the previous implementation, 4,096 independent branches improve by a median 7.824x and a wide retained frontier by 1.950x across three seeds; the small frozen groups remain near parity. Revised planning is faster than local search in 212/230 conditions and scenario exact in 217/230, so neither comparison is a universal win. Storage parity with the manual optimum remains unchanged.

## Complete recovery census on 26 October

The [complete recovery census](../results/research/recovery_census_oct26/RESULTS.md) supersedes the earlier 36-history native latency result as the broader evidence.
At equal optimal paid payloads, all 864 fixed configurations exceed a 1.10x DVC/RecoverML median time ratio.
Median paired speedups are 1.6952x for CLI and 48.3212x for warm API, with 7,776/7,776 exact method trials.
Every dataset, recipe, loss, request-size and interface stratum passes the prespecified 90% gate.
The initial checker ordering failure and the successful verification-only repair are both documented; original timings were not rerun.

The direct executor provides an explicit negative control: RecoverML reaches 1.10x speedup in 21/864 comparisons and is at least 1.10x slower in 48/864.
Its median ratio is near parity, slightly favoring the direct control.
The demonstrated advantage is reduced orchestration cost relative to the tested native DVC integration, not a faster general replay engine.
This authored finite catalogue cannot establish recovery wins in 90% of unknown production workloads.
Complete native HYPPO/HELIX lifecycle comparisons and universal dominance remain unestablished; manual optimum storage parity remains.

## Lossless storage follow-up on 26 October

The [complete lossless-store experiment](../results/research/encoded_storage_oct26/RESULTS.md) preserves original artifact identities while optimizing actual encoded file lengths.
All 216 fresh conditions improve on raw exact storage by at least 35.96%, with a 71.83% median reduction.
Adaptive exact completes all 504 requests. Six robust controls complete all 3,024 requests; the three adapters contribute 216 expected blocks and no unexpected outcomes.
The stronger shuffled-gzip control was subsequently given raw fallback and reoptimized without changing measured requests.
Adaptive storage remains smaller in all 216 comparisons, with 9.80% median saving, but only 106/216 savings reach 10%.
Thus the stronger 10%-gain / 90%-frequency claim is not established against compressed storage.

Same-codec local search ties 213/216 conditions and loses by at most 1.45% in the other three.
Same-codec snapshots tie all 72 broad-loss conditions. The supplied manual optimum ties every exact byte result.
These facts rule out an all-baseline storage-dominance claim even though the encoded store is materially smaller than the old raw representation.
All 230 older conditions also remain reported: 228 complete byte-level objectives, one selected-only Letter measurement and one unavailable Letter selected store.
That transfer is not counted as new model replay, and standard compression is not claimed as a new optimization invention.
