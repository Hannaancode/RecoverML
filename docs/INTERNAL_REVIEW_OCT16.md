## Assessment

There is a substantive executable artifact, a clear conditional recovery specification, substantial preserved measurements, two checked exact formulations, and unusually useful negative controls. ## Definition and proof review

The contract clearly distinguishes logical operation nodes from captured physical bytes and protects a union of historical targets. The budget includes roots, metadata and contract costs. Grounded least closure correctly prevents aliases/cycles from manufacturing data. Complement closed-set cuts and certified termination are coherent within that model, and independently coded scenario expansion plus finite/exhaustive controls support the implementation.

The decomposition argument requires independent optional physical components after mandatory roots are fixed; shared optional artifacts must not be split. Shared failure labels do not alone invalidate the universal local obligations. A rooted single-artifact provider shortcut requires genuinely rooted producers, not a self-supporting alias. These conditions are visible in the implementation and should remain beside any theorem or optimization claim.

The single-producer DAG simplification is a useful boundary characterization. For every declared group being individually eligible to fail and k>=1, universal availability follows the all-group-disabled reasoning device. The resulting failure paths admit an ordinary weighted vertex-cut formulation. This does not extend automatically to OR alternatives, unsupported outage families or ungrounded cycles. Our structural classifier is conservative and is tested on those counterexamples. No general new min-cut algorithm is claimed.

This is an internal argument review and finite verification, not a proof-assistant certificate. Runtime exactness also depends on the completeness and correctness of capture/operator contracts. Solver status and numeric postchecks establish results only within the encoded objective and tolerances.
