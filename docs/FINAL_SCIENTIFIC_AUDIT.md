# RecoverML scientific audit and submission decision

Review date: 16 October 2026. This is an internal parallel AI review of the implementation and saved evidence. It is not an external human review or a guarantee of publication. Paper drafting and formatting are deferred at the author's request.

**Decision: NOT YET READY for a strong external submission claim.** There is a working system with substantial checked evidence. The full scientific and retinal studies and the larger storage experiment now pass their independent reconciliation and the new raw evidence release is being completed. More fundamentally, strong algorithmic originality and a practical need for the general optimizer remain unresolved. Completing a large storage experiment alone cannot settle those questions.

This checklist follows the author's 24-task validation plan supplied on 15 October. PASS means the stated, bounded acceptance check is supported. PARTIAL means useful work exists but the full task is incomplete. BLOCKED identifies work needing unavailable evidence or an author decision. Historical review documents retain their original dates and may describe gaps that later evidence resolves; this report states the current scope explicitly.

## Strongest supported findings

1. The system can check exact historical recovery under its explicit byte identity and bounded capability-loss contract. Physical content addressing charges one retained payload once; roots and manifest and contract metadata are included.
2. The original published-component comparison recovers 3,344/3,344 requests with RecoverML, versus 2,291 for tested HYPPO frequency and 2,240 for HYPPO rank and 1,940 for the best tested common-budget HELIX setting. These are released-component/rule comparisons through a shared executor, not universal native-system wins.
3. The completed [Letter study](../results/research/gigabyte_letter_oct16/RESULTS.md) records **3,664,716,552 canonical bytes saved** from **3,669,545,821** snapshot bytes to **4,829,269** retained bytes, or **99.8684%** (3.6647 decimal GB). Actual gzip saving is **290,607,161 bytes**, below one GB compressed. Broad training/prediction loss saves zero. Both robust heuristics tie preprocessing exact storage. This supersedes the earlier 19,986,511-byte merged-history maximum; it does not imply a new optimization advantage.
4. Broadening the fault contract can eliminate storage savings because fitting and prediction can both become unavailable. Exact retention minimizes stored bytes and does not automatically minimize recovery latency.
5. Sensitivity and ablations identify limits as well as mechanisms: direct heuristic ties are meaningful, equal-output alternatives add no payload benefit in the four new real-recipe controls, and ordinary indexing/decomposition are established techniques.
6. A fresh compact protocol works on a distinct cloud environment without disabling strict checks. That is stronger than merely creating another virtual environment on the original host, but narrower than portable replay of every old captured blob.

## Novelty and baseline verdicts

The three defensible contribution categories are a precise historical-recovery contract, a checked system and exact specializations for that contract, and a reproducible study of retention/recompute tradeoffs. [Plain contribution statement](CONTRIBUTIONS_OCT15.md). These are not three confirmed new mathematical inventions.

The general weighted master/oracle architecture is established implicit hitting-set/Horn-abduction machinery. The single-producer acyclic subclass is standard weighted vertex cut and includes many current real-recipe histories. The retinal topology admits an even simpler shared-frontier rule. Large files and many versions do not establish combinatorial necessity. Stronger optimization-necessity claims need independently motivated interacting alternatives or must be narrowed.

The current baseline characterization is honest if it keeps component/rule and native-system results separate. The native DVC pipeline reproduction control is complete and its manually preserved feature frontier matches the exact payload minimum at 2,523,881 common bytes. Such a tie should be reported as evidence that RecoverML automates a valid retention choice rather than evidence that no other tool can implement it.

The strong heuristic and manual DVC ties also remain part of the conclusion.
