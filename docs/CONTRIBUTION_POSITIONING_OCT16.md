## Recommended position

Position RecoverML as **auditable retention planning for exact historical ML artifacts under explicitly declared replay-capability loss**, supported by a checked implementation and an empirical account of when selective retention helps and when simple methods suffice.

The research question is: **Which captured files must remain stored so that every protected historical output can still be recovered, given specified processing capabilities that may no longer execute?** The useful distinction is the preservation obligation and its accounting/verification boundary. Its universal quantifier is a feasibility requirement; it does not imply a new general optimization architecture.

The decision is to use a systems-and-evidence contribution, with exactness arguments attached to a precise model. Do not lead with a claim that the general integer optimizer is necessary for the tested applications. Do not lead with new classifier accuracy, universal superiority, or the largest uncompressed number alone. This is a positioning decision supported by current evidence; originality against all literature remains unestablished.

## Three defensible contributions

| Contribution | What is supported now | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| C1. Operational prevalence of this failure model is not measured. |
| C2. A checked executable retention service | Two separately coded exact formulations, root-conditioned components, grounded recovery, proof-status handling and output-hash checks implement the contract. Simple subclasses and failure boundaries are documented. | New implicit hitting-set machinery, new worklist indexing, an externally independent implementation, or a general need for MILP. Certification remains conditional on the model, complete dependencies, intact files, numerical environment and solver tolerances. |
| C3. Measured storage/recovery boundaries | Fixed public-data and real-image histories quantify storage, recovery coverage, CPU/time cost, representation effects, stronger-loss outcomes, and strong-baseline ties. Original negative and interrupted evidence is retained. | Widespread production savings, population significance, superiority on all objectives, or one GB saved in compressed storage. |

The integration is a candidate application contribution. Correctness and engineering completeness do not by themselves establish research novelty. These three categories should not be described as three independent new mathematical inventions.

## Best next steps and decision gates

1. **For the current contribution:** lead with the contract, checked system and tradeoff evidence. Use the structural scorecard to explain why exact certification and simple choices can coexist. The complete final reports belong alongside the older draft when requesting review.
2. **For a stronger optimization paper:** acquire independently motivated workflows with interacting alternative producers and demonstrate a material same-contract advantage over strong heuristics or a useful specialization with evidence. Freeze acceptance metrics before evaluating them. Larger easy forests alone do not answer this question. This is new research, not an already achieved contribution.
3. **For a stronger systems paper:** validate annotation/capture effort and deployment behavior with real users/workflows, and measure recovery deadlines and operating costs if claiming operational value. No human-effort benefit or production economics is currently measured.
4. **Before submission:** resolve the closest full-paper gaps, obtain an independent expert challenge, close or explicitly disclose the original frontier-container provenance gap, and integrate the final results into the manuscript in a subsequent revision.

No venue acceptance probability is assigned. The internal positioning and claim-selection task is complete for this evidence base. External novelty assessment, stronger operational evidence and manuscript integration remain open.

## Delta storage evidence added on 27 October

The [full delta follow-up](../results/research/delta_storage_oct27/RESULTS.md)
adds exact byte-range sharing while charging retained base dependencies and
keeping storage decoding separate from ML replay. It lowers the old flat-format
minimum in 201/444 measurable conditions, with up to 39.53% less storage, and
ties the other 243. The two incomplete Letter objectives remain reported.
This extends the checked implementation using standard delta/compression and
optimization methods; it is not a new general codec or optimization theory.

The complete primary gate fails in all 72 strata. A stronger control retaining
local search's previous best plans leaves 22 wins and 422 ties. The largest
local gap uses RecoverML's preexisting flat optimum, so it is not a new 74%
delta-format gain. The same-format exact reference ties all measured optima,
and solid archives win 213 comparisons. Only one measured condition benefits
from changing the old exact retained set after enabling deltas. These boundaries
must accompany any new headline. The transfer checks canonical bytes and
conditional reachability; it does not add a full model-replay or latency census.

## Executable joint-compression follow-up, 27-28 October

The [solid-store follow-up](../results/research/solid_storage_oct27/RESULTS.md)
makes the earlier archive control executable with canonical-hash checks and
guarded replay. A five-candidate choice includes flat/delta optima and joint
compression of the flat-optimum and two local-search plans. This closes the
three storage losses revealed by offering local search the same encodings;
the original losses and the post-hoc correction remain published.

The 89.84% maximum reduction over the previous flat optimum is a specific
encoding benefit. The complete grid has a 0.094% median saving, 20 wins and
424 ties against equal-format local search, and a failed broad gate in all
72 primary strata. Established joint compression and heuristic fallback
strengthen the system, not a claim of a new optimizer or global compression
optimum. Whole-archive decoding and temporary replay files introduce costs;
no new speed or memory superiority is claimed.
